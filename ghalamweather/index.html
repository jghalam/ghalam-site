About GhalamWeather.

GhalamWeather is built around a simple idea: you shouldn't have to guess or convert between °F and °C. Every temperature in the app — current conditions, hourly, the 10-day forecast — shows both units at once, with whichever one you prefer displayed larger.

Beyond the basics, GhalamWeather adds a few things Joe Ghalam wanted from a weather app and couldn't quite find elsewhere: a live precipitation radar, real air quality data, moon phase and illumination, and a genuine barometric pressure trend read directly from your iPhone's own barometer — not just a number, but whether it's actually rising, falling, or holding steady.

Features.

🌡️

Dual Units, Always

Every temperature shows °F and °C together — no digging through Settings to check the other unit.

🗺️

Live Radar

An animated precipitation radar map, so you can see rain moving in before it arrives.

🌙

Moon Phase

Phase, illumination percentage, moonrise and moonset, and a countdown to the next full moon.

📊

Real Barometric Trend

Reads your device's own barometer over time to show whether pressure is rising, falling, or steady.

💨

Air Quality & Wind

Current AQI with a plain-language description, plus wind speed, gusts, and direction on a compass dial.

📱

Home & Lock Screen Widgets

Widgets in every size, including Lock Screen — and they keep updating as you travel, even when the app isn't open.

🌧️

Next-Hour Rain Intensity

An optional minute-by-minute rain graph for the next hour, using your own free OpenWeatherMap key.

🌐

English & French

Fully localized — the app follows your device's language and region automatically.

🔒

No Account, No Ads

No sign-up, no analytics, no advertising. Your weather data stays on your device.
