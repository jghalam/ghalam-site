About InvoiceMoi.

InvoiceMoi is a bilingual (French/English) invoice and estimate builder. Fill in your letterhead, company and client details, add as many sections and line items as the job needs, set your tax lines, and generate a polished PDF — all in the browser, nothing uploaded anywhere.

InvoiceMoi is also available as an iOS app, adding optional iCloud sync across devices, saved company profiles and customers, invoice status tracking (Open → Billed → Paid), and billing summary reports — while keeping the same no-account, your-data-stays-yours philosophy. Features.

🧾

Invoice or Estimate

One toggle switches every label — headings, footers, filenames — between Invoice and Estimate, in either language.

🌐

Bilingual

Full French/English support, including number formatting, dates, and the generated PDF itself.

📐

Custom Sections

Add unlimited sections and line items — descriptions, quantities, units, and prices, totaled automatically.

💱

Any Currency Format

Set your currency symbol, position, and separator style — applied everywhere in the document.

📎

Attach Terms

Append an existing terms/legal PDF directly onto the generated invoice.

🔒

Private by Design

The web app runs entirely in your browser. No servers, no accounts, no tracking.
