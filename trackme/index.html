About TrackMe.

TrackMe is a GPS tracker built for the kind of trips that take more than one go: a mountain bike ride, a backcountry hike, a road trip that covers several days. Start a recording, put the phone away, and TrackMe keeps tracking with the screen off. Stop for the night, and pick the same journey up the next morning — the stretches you didn't record, like a flight or a drive to the trailhead, are shown as a dotted line so the whole trip still reads as one story.

It also works as a handy dashboard while you move, with a big live speedometer, altitude and compass heading, plus a G-force meter and a Launch timer for the car, with 0–60, quarter-mile and braking runs. Maps come from Apple Maps, and everything you record stays on your device — there is no account, no ads and no analytics.

Features.

📍

Background Recording

Keeps recording with the screen off or while you use other apps, with a safe-save that protects your track if the app is ever closed unexpectedly.

🔁

Save & Continue Journeys

Reopen any saved journey and continue it later. Each session becomes a colored segment, and unrecorded gaps show as dotted lines. Small arrows show which way you went.

🚴

Built for Your Activity

Mountain bike, road bike, hike, walk, run, travel or other, each with sensible speed, pace and auto-pause settings.

🗺️

Live Map

Follow yourself with a heading beam, auto-zoom with speed, and a smooth route line. Earlier segments of the journey stay visible in another color.

📸

Waypoints with Notes & Photos

Mark a place with a name, photos, who you were with and notes. Photos are one tap with the built-in camera, can be cropped and marked up, and can be shared from the full-screen view. Waypoints are numbered along your route, and you can add more to saved journeys by tapping the path.

🎛️

Live Dashboard

A big speedometer dial, altitude and compass heading, with boxes you can swap around. Tap any box to hear its value read aloud.

🎯

G-Force Meter

A target that shows acceleration, braking and cornering in green, red and orange, with live and peak values. Works with the phone mounted upright or lying flat, and the readings are smoothed so bumps don't make them jump.

🏁

Launch Timer

Time 0–60 mph (or 0–100 km/h), the quarter and half mile, rolling races such as 30–70, and braking from any speed you choose. A Christmas-tree start, live G-force, and a results list with the details of every run. For closed courses only.

🌈

Speed-Colored Routes

See a saved journey colored from red for your slowest stretches to green for your fastest, with a legend and a switch to turn it off. Drag to make the map as large as you like.

🔊

Spoken Markers

Optional voice updates at every mile or kilometer, or every few minutes, with the time, pace or speed you choose. Set the interval and what is said in Settings.

🔋

GPS Profiles

Choose Precise, Balanced or Battery Saver, or set your own point spacing, GPS mode and bad-fix limit with Custom.

📈

Raw Sensor View

Watch the motion sensors live and record them to a CSV file for your own analysis. Handy for tinkering and learning how the sensors behave.

🏝️

Lock Screen & Dynamic Island

See your time, distance and speed at a glance without unlocking your phone.

⏸️

Auto-Pause

Pauses when you stop and resumes when you move, with a speed threshold you set for each activity.

🌙

Auto Night Mode

A dark, dimmed look with a red accent from 20 minutes before sunset until sunrise (or by screen brightness, if you prefer), so the screen isn't glaring on night rides and drives.

📤

Export & Backup

Export tracks as GPX, including speed and direction for every point, or make a full backup file with waypoints and photos that you can import back later. You always get a confirmation when it's done.

🔒

No Account, No Ads

No sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no cloud. Your tracks, notes and photos stay on your device.
